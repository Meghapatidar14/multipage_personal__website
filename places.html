MY TRAVEL DIARY

Favourite Destinations ✨

Exploring new places is one of my favourite activities. I love destinations that offer natural beauty, peace, and memorable experiences.

01
🌿 Nature & Waterfalls

Pachmarhi

Madhya Pradesh, India

Pachmarhi is a beautiful hill station known for its greenery, waterfalls, and peaceful environment.

02
🚤 Peace & Boating

Sailani Island

Madhya Pradesh, India

Sailani Island is a beautiful tourist destination located on the backwaters of Narmada river, famous for its scenic natural beauty, boating and peaceful surroundings.

03
🏛️ Heritage & Culture

Maheshwar

Madhya Pradesh, India

Maheshwar is a historic town situated on the banks of Narmada river. It is famous for beautiful forts, ancient temples, ghats, and traditional Maheshwari sarees.
